## FOUNDATIONAL AWARENESS PROGRAM

**For commanders, senior NCOs, S2s, and unit leadership.**

FFP-101 is the leadership-level entry point to the Force Protection track. Where FFP-201 builds hands-on operator capability over five days, FFP-101 gives leadership the threat landscape context they need to understand what their personnel face, what organic capability looks like, and how to resource a unit-level personal security program.

This is the course that answers "why does this matter?" before an organization commits to sending operators through FFP-201.

### What FFP-101 Covers

- The personal digital threat landscape, what adversaries collect, how they collect it, and who is targeted
- How commercial devices, applications, and behaviors create exploitable signatures
- What FFP-201 produces in an operator and what an organic unit capability looks like
- How to assess unit digital exposure at a leadership level without a technical background
- How to resource and sequence a unit-level personal security training program

### Who Should Attend

- Commanders and senior NCOs evaluating FFP-201 for their units
- S2s and personnel security officers building a unit security posture
- Unit leadership preparing personnel for deployment to non-permissive or FIS-heavy environments
- Law enforcement supervisors and investigators responsible for personnel digital security

**No prerequisites. DoD/government sponsorship required.**
